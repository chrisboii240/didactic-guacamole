Why parts sit where they do

  • Pi 4 behind the screen, with its USB ports facing up. That keeps the Flipper's cable short.
  • Battery next to the Pi, in the thicker screen part. The keyboard part stays thin.
  • The keyboard sets the width. It's 6 in wide, so the body can't get much narrower. Try the 5" button to see this.
  • Lean back tilts the screen part back from the keyboard. Your sketch's "slight angle" looks best at about 6–10°.
  • Two 2" screens (top box and AI pod) each have their own small case. Both would connect to the Pi's GPIO pins with short ribbon cables.

Before buying the second 2" screen, check that both screens can share the Pi's GPIO pins.
